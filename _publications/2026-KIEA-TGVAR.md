abstract: >-
  This paper develops an exact estimator for country-specific thresholds in Global VAR country blocks using a complete observed-value candidate grid, a frozen diagonal scale matrix, candidate-by-candidate unpenalized OLS, a deterministic lower-tie rule, and fail-closed validity checks. Under fixed non-zero threshold effects, it establishes consistency and T-rate tightness for threshold locations and first-order oracle transfer for the full coefficient vector. Under shrinking effects, it derives a joint limit for the rescaled threshold location and criterion supremum within a restricted invariant Gaussian subclass. Monte Carlo experiments and a 33-country external-debt application document both favorable and adverse finite-sample patterns; the analysis does not provide a test for threshold existence or identify causal tipping points.
